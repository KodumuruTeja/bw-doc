How BillWallet® Works

From setup to paid in seconds

1

One-Time Registration

Customers sign up using just an email. No account numbers or passwords required.

2

Secure Verification

One Time Code (OTC) sent to email verifies identity. BillWallet® ID is issued — works across all channels.

3

Link Bills Once

Users link billing accounts and payment methods once. BillWallet® remembers everything.

4

Pay in Seconds

Returning users get Express Payment (one-click). Standard users confirm via OTC. Under 30 seconds.
