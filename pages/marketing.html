  • "What percentage of your payments come through guest pay vs. registered portal?" — If >40% guest pay, BillWallet is a clear win.

  • "What channels do your customers use to pay?" — If multi-channel (web + IVR + agent), BillWallet unifies the experience.
  • "What's your average payment completion time?" — BillWallet brings this under 30 seconds for returning users.

FAQ Section (for biller help center)

What is BillWallet?

BillWallet is a free, secure service that saves your payment details so you can pay your bill in one click next time. No passwords, no apps to download.

How do I sign up?

Just check the "Save to BillWallet" box after making your next payment. Your account number, payment method, and contact info will be saved securely.

Is it safe?

Yes. BillWallet uses bank-level encryption (PCI Level 1 certified), one-time verification codes instead of passwords, and only works on approved websites.

Do I need to download an app?

No. BillWallet works directly on the payment website. Nothing to download or install.

How do I pay next time?

When you return to pay, the BillWallet widget will appear. Confirm your identity (or just click "pay" if you're a returning user on the same device), and you're done in seconds.

What if I want to use a different payment method?

You can manage your payment methods, add new ones, or switch your default through the BillWallet widget or portal.

Does it cost anything?

No. BillWallet is completely free for customers.
