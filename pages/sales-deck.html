Slide 9 — AutoPay: Removing the Last Barrier

Why don't more customers enroll in AutoPay?

  1. They don't trust storing payment info on "yet another" website
  2. The enrollment flow requires too many steps
  3. They want control over when/how much is paid

BillWallet® solves all three:

  1. Trust — One verified identity with enterprise-grade security; not another website to manage
  2. Simplicity — Payment method is already linked; AutoPay enrollment is a single toggle
  3. Control — BillWallet® can present bill details, usage alerts, and amount before auto-debit
Higher AutoPay enrollment = predictable cash flow + lower cost-to-serve.
