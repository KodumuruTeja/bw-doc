BillWallet® is Paymentus' patented, AI-native digital wallet that transforms how consumers pay bills — delivering 4x faster payments, reducing abandonment by up to 80%, and increasing on-time payment rates across all channels. For billers, this translates directly into accelerated cash flow, lower cost-to-serve, and measurably higher customer satisfaction.

## The Problem Billers Face Today

### Payment Abandonment Is Costing You Revenue

| Challenge | Impact |
|-----------|--------|
| **80% of bill payments are abandoned at login** | Lost revenue, increased DSO, higher call center volume |
| **60% of payments are one-time (guest) payments** | Customers re-entering details every month — friction leads to late/missed payments |
| **Average household pays 12–15 bills/month** | Consumers manage dozens of credentials — yours gets forgotten |
| **9+ billion manual account entries annually (U.S.)** | Every manual entry is a chance for error, abandonment, or fraud exposure |

### The Root Cause: Fragmented Identity

Traditional billing portals require consumers to remember biller-specific usernames, passwords, and account numbers. Each forgotten credential becomes:
- An unpaid bill
- A frustrated phone call ($5–$12 per call center interaction)
- A customer at risk of switching providers

---

## The BillWallet® Solution

### One ID. Every Channel. Every Bill. Paid in Seconds.

BillWallet® replaces fragmented, password-based authentication with a **single, verified digital identity** that works across web, mobile, IVR, voice assistant, and agent-assisted channels.

### How It Works (Consumer Experience)

1. **One-Time Registration** — Customer signs up with just an email. No account numbers. No passwords.
2. **Identity Verification** — Paymentus verifies the consumer and issues a unique BillWallet® ID.
3. **Link Bills & Payment Methods Once** — Consumer connects their billing accounts and preferred payment methods.
4. **Pay in Seconds** — Every future payment requires only confirming the BillWallet® ID. One click. Done.

## Business Value for Billers

### 1. Accelerate Cash Flow

| Metric | Traditional Portal | With BillWallet® |
|--------|--------------------|------------------|
| Payment completion time | 2–5 minutes | Under 30 seconds |
| Login abandonment rate | ~80% | Near-zero (no passwords) |
| On-time payment rate | Industry average | Measurably higher through friction removal |

**Result:** Faster payments = reduced Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) and improved working capital.

### 2. Reduce Cost-to-Serve

- **Fewer password-reset calls** — No passwords means no resets. Each avoided call saves $5–$12.
- **Lower IVR/agent handle time** — BillWallet® identity works on IVR/voice; no need to verify account numbers verbally.
- **Reduced paper bill costs** — Higher digital engagement drives paper suppression.

### 3. Increase Digital Adoption & AutoPay Enrollment

- **Frictionless first payment** leads to higher AutoPay conversion (consumers who pay easily once are more likely to set up recurring).
- **Omnichannel consistency** — Same BillWallet® ID works on portal, mobile, IVR, and agent desktop — consumers aren't stuck on a single channel.
- **Persistent relationship** — Unlike one-time guest pay, BillWallet® creates a lasting digital relationship that increases engagement over time.

### 4. Improve Customer Satisfaction (CSAT/NPS)

- Consumers report that payment ease is a top driver of utility/service provider satisfaction.
- **4x faster payments** directly correlate with higher satisfaction scores.
- Reduced friction = fewer complaints, fewer escalations, better brand perception.

### 5. Enhanced Security Posture

- **Passwordless architecture** — Nothing to phish, steal, or brute-force.
- **Verified identity** — Every BillWallet® ID is identity-proofed before issuance.
- **PCI Level 1 compliant** — Enterprise-grade encryption for all stored data.
- **Reduced data entry** — Less manual input = fewer interception opportunities.
- **SecureService™ framework** — End-to-end protection across all payment flows.
